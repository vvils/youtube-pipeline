YouTube Pipeline

A private command-line tool its owner uses to publish their own animated videos to their own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video, with its title, description and thumbnail, to a channel its owner manages.
  • Reads that channel's YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, audience retention), so the owner can see where viewers stopped watching and improve the next video.

Who can use it

Only its owner. It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up for it, and it never asks anyone else to sign in.

Google and YouTube

It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your data under the Google Privacy Policy.
